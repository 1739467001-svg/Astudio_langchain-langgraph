# 离线批量推理管线图解

> 分块→并发Worker→检查点→断点续跑。中断后不重头。

---

```mermaid
graph TB
    DATA["数据集"] --> CHUNK["分块N条/批"]
    CHUNK --> QUEUE["任务队列"]
    QUEUE --> W1["Worker1"]
    QUEUE --> W2["Worker2"]
    QUEUE --> W3["Worker3"]
    W1 & W2 & W3 --> RESULT["结果写入"]
    RESULT --> CKPT["检查点<br/>每50条保存"]
    CKPT -->|"中断"| RESUME["断点续跑"]

    style CHUNK fill:#FFF9C4,stroke:#F9A825,stroke-width:3px
    style CKPT fill:#E3F2FD
    style RESUME fill:#C8E6C9
```

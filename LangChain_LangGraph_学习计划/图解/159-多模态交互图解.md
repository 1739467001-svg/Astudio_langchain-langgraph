## 三、输出生成

```mermaid
graph TB
    RESPONSE["LLM回答"] --> NEED_IMG{"需图像?"}
    NEED_IMG -->|是| GEN_IMG["DALL-E生成"]
    NEED_IMG -->|否| NEED_AUDIO{"需语音?"}
    NEED_AUDIO -->|是| TTS["TTS生成"]
    NEED_AUDIO -->|否| TEXT_OUT["文字输出"]

    style GEN_IMG fill:#E3F2FD
```

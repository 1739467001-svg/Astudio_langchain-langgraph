# 降级链与断路器图解

> gpt-4o熔断→gpt-4o-mini→gpt-3.5→缓存兜底。断路器三态：CLOSED/OPEN/HALF_OPEN。

---

```mermaid
graph TB
    REQ["请求"] --> CB1{"断路器: gpt-4o"}
    CB1 -->|CLOSED| M1["gpt-4o"]
    CB1 -->|OPEN| SKIP["跳过"]
    M1 -->|成功| OK["返回"]
    M1 -->|失败| TRIP["熔断"]
    SKIP & TRIP --> CB2{"断路器: mini"}
    CB2 -->|成功| OK
    CB2 -->|失败| CACHE["缓存兜底"]

    style CB1 fill:#FFF9C4,stroke:#F9A825,stroke-width:3px
    style OK fill:#C8E6C9

```

---

## 断路器三态

| 状态 | 行为 | 转换条件 |
|------|------|----------|
| CLOSED | 正常放行 | 失败N次→OPEN |
| OPEN | 熔断跳过 | 超时→HALF_OPEN |
| HALF_OPEN | 试探1个 | 成功→CLOSED, 失败→OPEN |
